How it works on-chain

No company in the middle. Just rules in code.

Your Safe is a program on Solana and a contract on Ethereum-style networks. This page says exactly what that code allows, who can do what, and what can still change.

A withdrawal, step by step

  1. You ask. Your Safe records the amount, where it goes, and when it may leave: after your delay (24 hours on Gentle, 72 on Strong), and never before a freeze ends.
  2. You're warned. The wallet shows it, and Telegram or email alerts tell you even when you're away.
  3. You can cancel the whole time. One tap, and the money simply stays in your Safe. Nothing is lost but a tiny network fee.
  4. When the time comes, it runs. Anyone may trigger it, but the money can only go to the address you chose. Nobody gains anything by triggering it.

Two exceptions, both chosen by you. Sends to an address you trusted earlier are instant, but still count toward your daily limit and are still blocked during a freeze. And your guardian can release early a withdrawal you already asked for, in an emergency. A guardian can't start a withdrawal, change its amount or change where it goes.

Rules that only loosen slowly

Anything that makes your Safe harder to empty applies at once: a longer delay, a lower daily limit, a freeze. Anything that makes it easier waits the rule delay, 3 days on Gentle and 7 on Strong, and never applies before a freeze ends. You can cancel a pending change the whole time.

So a thief with your recovery phrase can't shorten your delay, add their own address or raise your limit in a hurry. Every way out takes time, and you see it coming.

What Ecrin itself can't do

No admin key

Neither the Solana program nor the Ethereum contract has an owner, admin or operator role.

No pause switch

Nobody can freeze everyone's funds, or yours. Only you can freeze your own Safe.

No fees taken from the Safe

The code has no fee and no treasury. You only pay the network's own fee.

Works without us

The website and wallet are only windows onto the chain. If they disappeared, your Safe and its rules would still work from any tool.

What can still change

Ethereum contract: fixed

It has no upgrade mechanism. The code deployed is the code that runs, for good.

Solana program: upgradeable while testing

Solana programs can be upgraded by the key that deployed them. Before mainnet, after the audit, that key moves to a multi-person key, and later the program is made permanent.

Not audited yet

An independent audit, with the report published here, comes before any real money. Test coins only until then.

Bug bounty at mainnet

Until then, report flaws privately from the security page.

Built-in limits

  • Up to 8 trusted addresses, 4 waiting withdrawals and 4 waiting rule changes per Safe.
  • A freeze can last at most 10 years, so a typo or a thief can't lock your money forever.
  • The daily limit counts everything that leaves in one UTC day, trusted sends included.

The official program and contract addresses are on the security page. Check them before you sign.
