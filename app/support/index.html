Quick answers

I can't get test coins

Open Ecrin Wallet on Testnet and tap Get free test SOL: it gives 0.25 test SOL once a day. If Ecrin's faucet is empty or busy, the wallet opens Solana's own faucet with your address copied. For other networks, the Receive screen links to each network's faucet.

My withdrawal hasn't arrived

That's the Safe working: every withdrawal waits the delay you chose (24 hours, 72 hours…), and longer if your Safe is locked. The Safe screen shows its countdown. When it ends, open the Safe and finish it. Until then you can cancel it.

Can Ecrin cancel or reverse a transaction for me?

No. Ecrin is self-custody: only your wallet can act on your Safe. You can cancel your own withdrawal while it waits. Money that has already left can't be brought back by anyone.

I lost my recovery phrase

Ecrin never has a copy, so no one can recover it for you. If your wallet is still open on a device, write the phrase down now from Settings → Recovery phrase, and move your funds to a new wallet if you think someone else saw it.

Is there an Ecrin token?

No. There is no token, contract address, presale or airdrop. Anything claiming otherwise is a scam.

Is Ecrin on mainnet?

The wallet works on mainnet (send, receive, swap). The Safe runs on test networks with free test coins, and comes to mainnet after an independent security audit.
