FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my money safe? Who holds it?

Your funds sit in a Safe on the network itself, tied to your wallet: a smart contract on Ethereum-style networks, a program on Sui. Ecrin never has your keys and can't move your funds. Only your wallet can request a withdrawal, and only the Safe's rules decide when it can run.

What if someone steals my key?

They can only ask to withdraw, and that waits your full delay, so cancel each request before it runs (Telegram and email alerts tell you within a minute). To move out, pay one of your trusted addresses, or request a withdrawal to a fresh wallet and have your guardian release it at once. A stolen key can make your rules stricter but never looser: it can't add its own trusted address, shorten a delay or name a guardian without waiting days, so at worst your funds are frozen, not taken. That's why we recommend a guardian on Strong.

What if I have a real emergency?

Your guardian can co-sign a withdrawal you already requested so it runs immediately. They can't start one, change the amount or pick where it goes. You can also always pay your trusted addresses instantly.

Can my guardian take my money?

No. A guardian can only approve something you asked for: an emergency release of your own withdrawal, or a trusted address you proposed.

Will I be able to earn yield?

That's the plan: native SOL staking where the stake stays inside the Safe, so you earn rewards without creating a fast way out. It comes after the audit.

What can I store?

Your Wallet holds coins on 13 networks: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Chain, Avalanche, HyperEVM, Tron, Sui and the XRP Ledger. The Safe holds each network's own coin and USDC on 8 of them, each with its own balance and daily limit under the same rules.

Does it cost anything?

The wallet is free. You pay the network's normal fees, and swaps include a 0.875% Ecrin fee, always shown before you confirm.

Is it live on mainnet?

Yes, in early access. The Wallet works on 13 mainnets and the Safe is open on 8 of them. It hasn't been audited yet, so start with small amounts. You can also try everything on test networks with free test coins.
