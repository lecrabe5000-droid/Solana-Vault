Crypto puts every exit one tap away.

A blocker app turns off in a minute. Staked coins unstake in hours, and liquid staking tokens sell instantly. When the urge, the scammer or the panic arrives, nothing stands in the way. Ecrin puts time between you and your savings, enforced by the blockchain itself.

Three ways out. Each one slower than an impulse.

  1. Deposit. Move SOL into your vault. It's a program account on Solana that only your wallet controls.

Features

Small rules. Real distance.

Cooldown

Every withdrawal waits out a delay you choose. Cravings and panics pass; your savings stay.

Guardian

A friend or family member is notified of every request and can approve an emergency. They can never move your money.

Trusted addresses

Up to 8 places money can go instantly. New ones take days to add, so a scammer can't sneak one in.

Lock until a date

Nothing leaves before the date you pick. Only a guardian-approved emergency can open it early.

Daily limit

Cap how much can leave in a day, trusted sends included. Lowering it is instant; raising it waits.

No receipt token

Unlike staking, the vault never hands you a token. There's nothing to sell to get around the wait.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my money safe? Who holds it?

Your funds sit in a program account on Solana tied to your wallet. Ecrin never has your keys and can't move your funds. Only your wallet can request a withdrawal, and only the program's rules decide when it can run.

What if I have a real emergency?

Your guardian can co-sign a withdrawal you already requested so it runs immediately. They can't start one, change the amount or pick where it goes. Without a guardian, the normal delay applies.

Can my guardian take my money?

No. A guardian can only approve something you asked for: an emergency release of your own withdrawal, or adding a trusted address you proposed.

What can I store?

SOL during the beta. USDC and other tokens are next.

Does it cost anything?

The devnet beta is free, using test SOL. You only pay normal Solana network fees, a fraction of a cent.

Is it live on mainnet?

Not yet. Ecrin is on Solana devnet while it's being tested. Mainnet comes after an independent security audit, with deposit caps at first.

Why "Ecrin"?

An écrin is the case that keeps something precious safe. That's the whole idea.
