The problem

On Solana, a transfer is final in under a second.

One bad signature, one leaked seed phrase, one person standing over you, and it's gone before you understand what happened. Every one of these attacks needs your money to move now. Ecrin is built so it can't.

Swipe

Wallet drainers

A fake mint or airdrop asks you to sign. Your vault isn't in your wallet's reach: the most a signature can do is ask to withdraw, and that waits.

Normal wallet: gone in one blockEcrin: queued, you cancel

How it works

Three ways out. Only your own addresses are fast.

Swipe
01

Trusted addresses

Your exchange account, your bank off-ramp, your cold wallet. Money sent here arrives instantly. Adding a new one waits days, or needs your guardian.

Instant
02

Any other withdrawal

Waits out your delay, from 1 hour to a year, before anyone can run it. You can cancel at any moment, for free.

Your delay
03

Loosening a rule

A shorter delay, a higher limit, a new trusted address, any guardian change: these wait days. Making rules stricter applies at once.

Days
  1. Create a vault. A program account on Solana that only your wallet controls. Ecrin never touches your keys.
  2. Pick your protection. Gentle, Strong or Locked, add your trusted addresses, and optionally a guardian.
  3. Live normally. Pay your own accounts instantly. Everything else waits, and you can always change your mind.
Features

A few strict rules. Enforced by the chain, not by us.

Swipe

Withdrawal delay

Every withdrawal to a new address waits out a delay you choose, with a live countdown you can watch.

One-tap cancel

Anything queued can be cancelled until the moment it runs. It costs a fraction of a cent and takes a second.

Trusted addresses

Up to 8 places your money can go instantly. New ones take days to add, so no one can sneak one in.

Guardian

Someone you trust can approve a real emergency early. They can never start a withdrawal or choose where money goes.

Lock until a date

Nothing leaves before the date you pick. Only a guardian-approved emergency opens it early.

Daily limit

Cap how much can leave in a day, trusted sends included. Lowering it is instant; raising it waits.

Security

The rules live on Solana, not in an app.

Close the website, delete the app, import your key somewhere else: the program still enforces every delay. Here is every shortcut we know of, and why it fails.

Way around itWhy it fails
Import your key into another walletThe key can only request a withdrawal. The program still enforces the wait.
Turn the rules offLoosening any rule waits days, and never before a lock ends.
Add the attacker's address as trustedNew trusted addresses wait days, or need your guardian's approval.
Name a second wallet as guardianAny guardian change waits days, so no one can approve their own withdrawal.
Sell a receipt token for instant SOLUnlike liquid staking, there is no token to sell.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my money safe? Who holds it?

Your funds sit in a program account on Solana tied to your wallet. Ecrin never has your keys and can't move your funds. Only your wallet can request a withdrawal, and only the program's rules decide when it can run.

What if someone steals my key?

They can only ask to withdraw, and that waits your full delay, so cancel each request before it runs (alerts that tell you right away are next on the roadmap). To move out, pay one of your trusted addresses, or request a withdrawal to a fresh wallet and have your guardian release it at once. A stolen key can make your rules stricter but never looser: it can't add its own trusted address, shorten a delay or name a guardian without waiting days, so at worst your funds are frozen, not taken. That's why we recommend a guardian on Strong.

What if I have a real emergency?

Your guardian can co-sign a withdrawal you already requested so it runs immediately. They can't start one, change the amount or pick where it goes. You can also always pay your trusted addresses instantly.

Can my guardian take my money?

No. A guardian can only approve something you asked for: an emergency release of your own withdrawal, or a trusted address you proposed.

Will I be able to earn yield?

That's the plan: native SOL staking where the stake stays inside the vault, so you earn rewards without creating a fast way out. It comes after the audit.

What can I store?

SOL on Solana devnet and ETH on Ethereum Sepolia during the beta. USDC and other tokens are next.

Does it cost anything?

The devnet beta is free, using test SOL. You only pay normal Solana network fees, a fraction of a cent.

Is it live on mainnet?

Not yet. Ecrin is on Solana devnet while it's being tested. Mainnet comes after an independent security audit, with deposit caps at first.
