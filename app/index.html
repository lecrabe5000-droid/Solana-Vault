FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my money safe? Who holds it?

Your funds sit in a program account on Solana tied to your wallet. Ecrin never has your keys and can't move your funds. Only your wallet can request a withdrawal, and only the program's rules decide when it can run.

What if someone steals my key?

They can only ask to withdraw, and that waits your full delay, so cancel each request before it runs (Telegram and email alerts tell you within a minute). To move out, pay one of your trusted addresses, or request a withdrawal to a fresh wallet and have your guardian release it at once. A stolen key can make your rules stricter but never looser: it can't add its own trusted address, shorten a delay or name a guardian without waiting days, so at worst your funds are frozen, not taken. That's why we recommend a guardian on Strong.

What if I have a real emergency?

Your guardian can co-sign a withdrawal you already requested so it runs immediately. They can't start one, change the amount or pick where it goes. You can also always pay your trusted addresses instantly.

Can my guardian take my money?

No. A guardian can only approve something you asked for: an emergency release of your own withdrawal, or a trusted address you proposed.

Will I be able to earn yield?

That's the plan: native SOL staking where the stake stays inside the vault, so you earn rewards without creating a fast way out. It comes after the audit.

What can I store?

During the beta: SOL and USDC on Solana devnet, and ETH (POL on Polygon) and USDC on Ethereum Sepolia, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism and Polygon test networks. Each coin has its own balance and daily limit under the same rules.

Does it cost anything?

The devnet beta is free, using test coins. You only pay normal network fees, a fraction of a cent.

Is it live on mainnet?

Not yet. Ecrin runs on test networks while it's being tested. Mainnet comes after an independent security audit, with deposit caps at first.
