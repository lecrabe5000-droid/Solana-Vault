The Safe

Two places for your money

Your Wallet spends instantly, like any wallet. Your Safe holds the rest. You decide how much lives in each, and you can move money into the Safe at any time, instantly.

The Safe lives on-chain

It's a smart contract on the network itself (a program on Sui), tied to your wallet. Not an app setting and not an account with us: Ecrin never holds your keys and can't move your money. Close Ecrin, import your key elsewhere, and the rules still hold.

Every withdrawal waits

Money leaving the Safe waits for the delay you chose: 24 hours, 72 hours, or nothing at all before a date you set. The countdown is public on-chain, and you can cancel it the whole time.

Choose how strong

Gentle waits 24 hours. Strong waits 72 hours and is recommended. Locked keeps everything in until your date. Your own trusted addresses, like your exchange or your cold wallet, are always paid instantly.

Cancel anything

Someone signs a withdrawal

Your key leaks, or a fake airdrop gets your signature. On any other wallet, the money is gone in seconds. Here the transaction reaches your Safe, and the Safe doesn't send it.

It's queued, with a countdown

The withdrawal waits on-chain for your full delay. Everyone can see the countdown, including you, and nothing can make it faster.

You're told within a minute

An alert reaches you by Telegram or email: amount, destination, time left. Not you? One tap.

Cancel. It never left.

Cancelling is free and instant. The money stays in your Safe, and you move out on your terms: to a trusted address, or to a fresh wallet with your guardian's help.

Scam shield

A site asks you to sign

Drainers dress up as airdrops, mints and support pages. Before anything is signed, Ecrin checks the site against known scam lists and blocks it before it can even ask.

You see what would leave

Every request is explained in plain words: exactly which coins would leave your wallet, and where to. No hex, no guessing.

Look-alike addresses flagged

Address poisoning copies the first and last characters of an address you use. Ecrin compares every address you send to and warns you, and it flags the first time you send anywhere new.

Sleep on it

The 3 a.m. decision

Gambling urges, revenge trades, panic sells. They all want the money now. Late at night, Ecrin offers to wait until morning before anything leaves.

By morning, it has passed

Urges fade in hours; your delay lasts days. Most withdrawals started in a hurry are cancelled the next morning, by the same person who started them.

Set it on a calm day

A daily limit (lowering is instant, raising waits 24 hours), a cooling-off pause before big sends, a lock until a date, and part of every payment saved automatically. Ecrin is a tool, not treatment: if gambling is hurting you, talk to someone you trust or a local helpline.

Rules that hold

A stolen key wants to loosen your rules

Shorten the delay, add its own trusted address, name itself guardian. Every one of these waits 7 days, in plain sight, and you can cancel it.

Tightening is instant

A longer delay, a lower limit, a lock: these apply immediately. So a key in the wrong hands can freeze your Safe, never empty it.

A guardian for real emergencies

Someone you trust can release a withdrawal you asked for, at once. They can never start one or choose where money goes, and changing guardians waits days too.

Open and honest

Every line is public

The wallet, the Solana program and the contracts are open source on GitHub, and tested in public on every change.

Check it yourself

The official contract and program addresses are published, so you can verify exactly what your Safe runs on. Found a flaw? Report it privately, so it's fixed before anyone else knows.

Not audited yet

The Safe is in early access on 8 mainnets. Keep amounts small until the independent audit is done and its report is public. A paid bug bounty follows the audit.

Questions

Who holds my money?

You do. It sits in a Safe on the network, tied to your wallet. Ecrin can't move it, freeze it or see your keys.

What if someone steals my key?

They can only ask to withdraw, and that waits your full delay. Cancel it. They can't loosen your rules without waiting days either.

Hardware wallet or Ecrin?

Both. A hardware wallet protects your key; Ecrin protects your money. Sign a drainer with a hardware wallet and it's gone instantly; with Ecrin you have time.

Does it cost anything?

The wallet is free. Normal network fees apply, and swaps include a 0.875% Ecrin fee. An écrin is the case that keeps something precious safe. That's the whole idea.
