How it works
  1. Create a Safe. An account on the network that only your wallet controls. Ecrin never touches your keys.
  2. Pick your protection. Gentle, Strong or Locked; add trusted addresses and, if you like, a guardian.
  3. Live normally. Pay your own trusted addresses instantly. Everything else waits, and you can always change your mind.

Loosening a rule (a shorter delay, a higher limit, a new trusted address, a new guardian) waits days. Tightening one is instant.

Security

Questions

Who holds my money? You do. It sits in a Safe on the network, tied to your wallet: a contract on Ethereum-style networks, a program on Sui.

What if someone steals my key? They can only ask to withdraw, and that waits your full delay. Cancel it. A stolen key can't add its own trusted address or guardian without waiting days.

What if I have a real emergency? Your guardian can release a withdrawal you asked for, at once. They can't start one or choose where it goes.

Does it cost anything? The wallet is free. You pay normal network fees, and swaps include a 0.875% Ecrin fee, shown before you confirm.

Why "Ecrin"? An écrin is the case that keeps something precious safe. That's the whole idea.
