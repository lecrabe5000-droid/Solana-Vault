The Safe

Your savings, guarded by time

Spend from your Wallet like any other. Keep the rest in your Safe, where every withdrawal waits for a delay you choose.

The Safe is an account on the network itself that only your wallet controls: a contract on Ethereum-style networks, a program on Sui. Ecrin never holds your keys and can't move your money. Your own trusted addresses, like your exchange or cold wallet, are paid instantly. Everything else waits.

Sleep on it

Protection from yourself

Gambling urges, revenge trades, panic sells. They all want the money now, and they fade in hours. Ecrin makes your money wait days.

Set your limits on a calm day; they hold when the urge hits. Late at night Ecrin offers to wait until morning, and if you ask, it warns you before a betting site connects. Ecrin is a tool, not treatment: if gambling is hurting you, talk to someone you trust or a local helpline.

Rules that hold

A stolen key can only make them stricter

Loosening any rule waits days. Tightening one is instant. So a key in the wrong hands can freeze your Safe, never empty it.

Choose a guardian for real emergencies: they can release a withdrawal you asked for, at once. They can never start one or choose where it goes, and any change of guardian waits days too.

Questions

Every answer, in plain words

Who holds my money?
You do. It sits in a Safe on the network, tied to your wallet. Ecrin can't move it.
What if someone steals my key?
They can only ask to withdraw, and that waits your full delay. Cancel it.
What about a real emergency?
Your guardian can release a withdrawal you asked for, at once, and trusted addresses are always instant.
How is it different from a hardware wallet?
A hardware wallet protects your key; Ecrin protects your money. Sign a drainer and a hardware wallet loses it instantly; Ecrin gives you time. They work best together.
Does it cost anything?
The wallet is free. Normal network fees apply, and swaps include a 0.875% Ecrin fee.
Why "Ecrin"?
An écrin is the case that keeps something precious safe. That's the whole idea.
